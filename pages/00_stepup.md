# What do you mean I'm doing a Year 12 subject?!!!

VCE Applied Computing is a Year 11 subject that is designed to prepare students for both Year 12 VCE Data Analytics and VCE Software Development.

Instead of offering the Year 11 subject, we offer both Year 12 subjects in alternating years. This helps us ensure we have enough students to run the classes, and also allows students to complete both subjects in their VCE if they wish.

If this is your first year of Applied Computing, we focus on getting you an S, with the exam and study score being a bonus. If you completed Software Development last year, then this is your big chance to get a strong study score in your VCE.

# What is Data Analytics about?

Data Analytics is about using data to make decisions. We will learn how to collect, clean, visualise and analyse data.

To do that, we will use:

- **Databases** to store, retrieve and filter data
- **Spreadsheets** to clean and analyse data
- **Python** to build interactive dashboards and visualisations

# Course Structure - Unit 3

Data Analytics has four outcomes, each of which is assessed through either a SAC or a SAT task.

::left::

### Outcome 1 - Data Analytics

- Identify, select, and extract authentic data.
- Manipulate data for visualisations and findings.
- Use database, spreadsheet, and visualisation tools.
- Test and validate results and findings.

**Assessment:** SAC. Set of practical laptop activities, completed in timed conditions in class.

::right::

### Outcome 2 - Analysis and Design

- Students identify a research question and collect data.
- Use primary and secondary data sources to inform requirements and scope.
- Create a project plan for the data analytics solution.
- Create multiple design ideas and evaluate against criteria to select a solution.

**Assessment:** SAT. Project Plan, data collection and design ideas. Completed in class and at home.

# Course Structure - Unit 4

::left::

## Outcome 1 - Development and Evaluation

- Develop a data analytics solution based on work in Unit 3.
- Test and evaluate solution against the requirements.
- Evaluate effectiveness of communication of findings and recommendations.
- Evaluate effectiveness of project plan and management of project.

**Assessment:** SAT. Visualisations, testing and evaluation. Completed in class and at home.

::right::

## Outcome 2 - Cyber Security: data security

- Analyse effect of cyber security threats on data integrity and privacy.
- Understand consequences for organisations for failing to protect data.
- Understand the legal and ethical responsibilities of organisations to protect data.

**Assessment:** SAC. Exam style questions in response to case-study of a data breach.

# The Exam

50% of your mark comes from the SAT and SAC tasks.

The other 50% comes from the end of year exam, which is a 2 hour written exam.

**To do well in Data Analytics you need to:**

- Complete all SAC and SAT tasks to the best of your ability
- Build a study routine that allows you to revise consistently across the year
- Practice exam questions as often as you can and get feedback on your answers
